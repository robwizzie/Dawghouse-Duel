How Dawg House Duel works

  1. Two dawgs sit facing the laptop. Each gets 45 seconds on their own clock.
  2. One picture at a time. Whoever has CONTROL is the only clock running.
  3. Name it right → the answer flashes up and control hands over to the other dawg with a fresh picture. Your clock stops.
  4. Wrong → 3 seconds off your clock, same picture, still your turn. Guess again.
  5. Stuck? Click the mouse to PASS. The answer is revealed and you get a different picture — but it costs you 2 seconds, and it is still your turn and still your clock.
  6. Only a correct answer gets you off the clock. First one to hit 00.0 loses.

Judging the answers — open the Host View

The answer key can’t live on the screen the dawgs are reading, so it gets its own screen showing the answer, the alternates that also count, and the next one up — plus big CORRECT / WRONG / PASS buttons.

On your phone: open the host URL shown on the setup screen, type the four-letter room code, and you’re in. Best setup for filming — the laptop faces the dawgs and you judge from your hand.

On the same computer: hit Host View on setup (or H mid-duel) for a second window. No pairing, no internet needed.

Either way the duel screen is the one running the game — if the phone drops off, the duel carries on and reconnects by itself.

Filming setup

Put the laptop just under the camera lens so the dawgs read the screen and look down the barrel. Give whoever has control the mouse — one click is a pass. Hit F for full screen before you roll.
